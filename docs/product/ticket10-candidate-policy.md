# Ticket 10 preview and candidate authorization policy

Ticket 10 follows the reference campaign contract without performing a remote mutation. A production preview requires the saved Personal discovery to report the selected Workspace role as `owner`, then performs two independent, complete read-only Workspace snapshots. Subscription expiry, paid default entitlement, per-seat-type occupancy, exact members, outbound invitations, member seat types and invitation seat types must agree across both snapshots and with the frozen Ticket 07 verification. Missing roles, untyped invitations, partial pagination, drift or stale evidence fail closed.

Original-member usage comes from the durable account×Workspace usage ledger; customer-delivery protection is account-global. `ever_used` is monotonic. `delivered` and `canceled_retired` retain the account permanently; `sale_reserved`, `delivery_pending`, `suspected_sold` and unknown evidence block authorization. A candidate needs an exact current invitation, `prolite` seat type, unique canonical identity, complete credentials and no global protection or blocking usage history. A successful complete read-only durable-ledger lookup may prove that no first-use row exists for the selected account×Workspace, producing scoped `unobserved_prejoin` evidence without writing zero or `never_used`. Existing `never_used` rows remain distinct observed facts; unknown/conflicting rows, historical `ever_used` in any Workspace and lookup errors fail closed. Original members with unknown usage remain excluded from replacement. The candidate is only `join_candidate_pending_first_probe`: never deliverable before a post-join, target-Workspace zero measurement is persisted.

Preview confirmation re-runs the independent evidence read, locks every durable writer epoch in deterministic order, re-reads all local facts, compares the canonical frozen scope, then records the exact Owner-selected slot→candidate mapping, evidence digest, session and epoch versions atomically. Same-key replay is idempotent; different facts or keys conflict; revocation prevents later use. Confirmation creates no invitation, join, removal, task, operation, Hub push or customer delivery. Future executors must recheck authorization, revocation and every frozen epoch before reserving a remote action.

Mock evidence remains available only to disposable integration tests. Its content-addressed absence ID checks fixture consistency, not production provenance. New frozen facts carry the current `policyVersion`; missing or non-current versions are displayed as `needs_verification` without rewriting historical facts or digests. Policy version 2 already distinguishes scoped complete-absence evidence from unknown usage; the official producer now supplies that existing contract. Confirmation rechecks ledger absence and global protection under the frozen writer epochs; old previews are neither upgraded nor rebased.
