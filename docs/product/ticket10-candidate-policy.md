# Ticket 10 candidate preview policy (mock-only)

The selected invited candidate can be a **controlled-join candidate**, not a deliverable account. A complete, fresh negative first-usage-record lookup in the typed mock evidence is `unobserved_prejoin`, not `never_used` and not a zero measurement. It is scoped to the selected canonical Workspace, exact account/identifier, mother session generation, current verification and selected account/credential/membership revisions. The lookup must explicitly report `complete` and `absent`, with its own mock ledger source/evidence ID. Missing, stale, partial, wrong-scope or failed probes remain unknown/excluded. Known ever-used history in retained mock previews (across Workspaces), global delivery/reservation/pending/suspected protection, unknown protection, wrong invitation/seat type and duplicate member identity block eligibility. Owner mapping is explicit and seat-type matched.

An eligible candidate is labeled `join_candidate_pending_first_probe` for delivery: **not deliverable**. Neither an invitation nor Personal account probe establishes target Workspace usage. No join or delivery executor, post-join scoped probe/zero ledger or durable cross-writer fence exists in this slice. The `deliverable` status is not emitted by Ticket 10. The mock absence lookup and preview-history sticky check are fixture assertions, **not** an authoritative production negative lookup or durable usage ledger; they must never authorize real join, sale or delivery. Production has no installed mock capability, and confirmation still returns `409 pending_write_fence` for a ready mock preview without remote calls. The post-join first measured and persisted zero evidence and production ledger/fence require separately reviewed implementation before any delivery grant.
